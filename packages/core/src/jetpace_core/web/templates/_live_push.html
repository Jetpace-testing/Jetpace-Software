{# What /partials/live actually answers with. THE PROBLEM. The design's first screen has the figures in a row at the top, the device table in the left column of the row below, and the facility and distribution panels in other cells entirely. All four want refreshing together, and they are not siblings - there is a trend chart between them that must NOT be swapped, because replacing a every five seconds destroys and redraws the chart under whoever is reading it. Four polled regions would be four requests, four connections and four sets of queries every five seconds, for one logical refresh. SO: one request, and the three panels that are not the target come back as out-of-band swaps. HTMX lifts any top-level element carrying `hx-swap-oob` out of the response and swaps it into the element with the same id, wherever that is in the document. The target region gets the rest. One round trip, one connection, four panels updated, and the chart between them untouched. Each out-of-band element has to carry its own tag, id and classes, because the swap replaces the whole element and not its contents. That is why the wrappers are repeated here and in index.html rather than being in the included partials. #} {% include "_live.html" %}
